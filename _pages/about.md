I'm an M.S. student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Kate Donahue. I'm interested in how people and AI systems make decisions together, and I combine theoretical models with empirical studies to understand and improve those decisions.

Right now I'm working on how multi-agent LLM coding teams should allocate skills and route work, and on belief aggregation in multi-LLM debate. With Prof. Chien-Ju Ho at Washington University in St. Louis, I study how much useful information AI explanations give people, using information bottleneck methods and human-subjects studies. I also work with Profs. Hari Sundaram and Karrie Karahalios on SeeQS, a tool for making sense of quadratic survey results.

Before my M.S., I received a B.S. in Mathematics and Computer Science from UIUC.

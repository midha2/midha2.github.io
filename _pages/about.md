I am a Master's student in Computer Science (Thesis-based; MSCS) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC). I am fortunate to be advised by [Kate Donahue](https://www.katedonahue.me).

I am interested in human-AI collaboration and AI-assisted decision-making. My master's work focuses on understanding and improving how people make decisions when interacting with multi-agent systems. I combine theoretical models with empirical studies to understand how people and AI agents can work well together.

I previously completed my B.S. in Mathematics and Computer Science at UIUC. During my undergrad, I also worked on explainable AI and quadratic surveys (papers coming soon!).
